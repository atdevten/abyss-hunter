# Epic: abyss-hunter

**Goal:** Lặn xuống vực thẳm tối đen, học cách sinh vật biển săn mồi, rồi săn chúng trước khi chúng săn bạn.

**Source:** `design/game-brief.md` (MVP list and Build order). No GDD or ADRs at the `minimal` tier.

**Scope:** the 7 MVP features. Out of scope as in the brief: story, multiplayer, crafting, base building, multiple seas, procedural maps, full ecosystem simulation, save system.

**Order:** the brief's Build order. The harpoon (MVP 4) comes before the species story because the shark fight needs it. Story 003 holds the riskiest proof: the same shark fight with and without encyclopedia knowledge.
